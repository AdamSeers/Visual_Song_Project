Visual Song

Upload a song and get back a video where every pitch is rendered as a colored shape. The colors aren’t arbitrary — each musical frequency is doubled into the visible light spectrum, so an A becomes a particular orange, an E becomes a violet, and so on, with all octaves of the same note sharing the same color. Louder notes are brighter; purer tones are more saturated, while voice or strings come out softer because of their overtones. Rounder shapes are mellow timbres, squarer ones are brighter timbres.

Photosensitivity warning: the output video contains rapidly changing colors and brightness. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing imagery, please avoid watching or proceed with caution.
